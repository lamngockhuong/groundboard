# Account page (synthetic fixture)

Copied from the product wiki on 2026-11-20. Frozen: edit only together with the boards.

## Reaching the page

The header of the product list links to the account page.

## What the page shows

The page shows the email the customer signs in with. The email cannot be changed here.

## Actions

"Change password" sends a reset link to that email; the password is never typed on this page.

"Delete account" removes the account after the customer confirms. What happens to orders that
are not delivered yet is still open: see the wiki page on account deletion.
